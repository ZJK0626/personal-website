Hello.

I'm Junke, a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the DE4M Lab, UT Dallas, advised by Prof. Liang He. Previously, I earned an M.S. in Computational Design from Carnegie Mellon University (2025) and a B.Arch. from Southeast University (2023).

Working at the intersection of design and technology, I reimagine physical environments as active, responsive media. While we spend ever more time in digital spaces, I believe the tangible world is what truly shapes our experiences. Drawing on computational design, tangible interaction, and ubiquitous computing in HCI, my work bridges the digital and the physical along three directions:

Spaces that Understand

Everyday environments and objects that sense, reason about, and proactively respond to people's needs through embedded intelligence.

People who Shape

AI-assisted participatory design and fabrication tools that empower non-experts to create and customize their physical surroundings.

Realities that Blend

Spatial computing that extends physical environments through context-aware virtual augmentation.

I'm always happy to chat about transformable furniture, digital fabrication, and tangible interaction — feel free to reach out.
